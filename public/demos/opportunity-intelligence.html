PROJECT BRIEF / 001

About this prototype

Purpose

Show how a bid inbox could help a procurement team turn incoming notices into structured, reviewable opportunities.

Intended audience

Bid managers, procurement analysts, proposal coordinators, and business development teams.

How it works

Five stages: intake, rule-based analysis, human bid/no-bid review, simulated handoff, and illustrative preliminary valuation. The logic is transparent and runs locally in your browser.

Try it yourself

  1. Select a fictional notice and click Analyze opportunity, or run all 15.
  2. Review the flagged risks and proposed next action.
  3. Set a human decision in the review queue.
  4. Explore the simulated assignments and illustrative value ranges.
  5. Reset the demo to start again.

Prototype scope

All names, notices, quantities, and rates are invented. No employer data, live AI, CRM access, automated bidding, or actual pricing approvals.

What this demonstrates

Opportunity qualification · Information architecture · Human-in-the-loop workflow · Data hygiene · AI-assisted prototyping
